## Common patterns
- Outcome-led headline, often with one coloured keyword; "AI-native" is the 2026 buzzword.
- Dark hero (near-black indigo/navy) with an abstract data or network visual; white body sections.
- Proof right under the hero: client logos, Clutch rating, "named #1 AI services company", partner badges (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, model providers).
- Services grouped by buyer journey: strategy → build → adopt/scale, plus industries.
- Named accelerators or packaged offers (PoC in 2 weeks, readiness assessment).
- Case studies with numbers; real leadership faces; webinars, podcasts and research articles.
- An AI assistant on their own site (HatchWorks).

## Recommended Purplease positioning
"AI agents and LLM apps, built on Go and cloud infrastructure that holds up in production."
Differentiator: most AI agencies stop at the demo; Purplease's Go, cloud and DevOps depth covers the production side (latency, cost, scaling, monitoring).
